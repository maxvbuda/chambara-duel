How to Duel

Player 1

  • WASD — Move (full 3D — strafe and step in/out)
  • Space — Jump
  • Move the mouse to aim your weapon — swing it in an arc to attack
  • Shift — Brace (block)

Player 2 / CPU

  • ↑↓←→ — Move (full 3D)
  • Right Shift — Jump
  • IJKL — Aim weapon (tap through directions to swing)
  • Ctrl — Brace (block)

There's no separate "attack" button — your weapon follows your aim like a real swing. The faster you whip it around, the harder it hits. The arena is fully 3D, so you can circle your rival, not just strafe left and right. Fall off the platform and your rival scores. Gamepads work too: left stick to move, right stick to swing, A to jump, shoulder button to brace.
